# Business Central: Environments and Companies

BC online distinguishes **production** and **sandbox** environments. Production handles daily business; sandboxes support learning, development, and testing. An environment can contain multiple companies, so discussions of data, permissions, or APIs should identify both the target environment and company.

Before development begins, record the environment name, purpose, owner, company list, source of test data, and applications connected to that environment.

References: [Production and sandbox environments](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/administration/environment-types) and [environment management](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/dev-itpro/administration/tenant-admin-center-environments).
