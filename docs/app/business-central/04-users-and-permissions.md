# Business Central: Users and Permissions

Access to BC depends on identity, licensing, and permissions within the application. **Permission sets** typically control which objects and operations users can access; projects must also check the user's company and business responsibilities.

When connecting a custom service to BC, answer separately: what can the end user see, what can the integration service call, and who may grant or change those permissions? Signing in to a custom application does not by itself grant access to BC data.

References: [Managing BC access](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/admin-access-overview) and [defining permissions](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/ui-define-granular-permissions).
