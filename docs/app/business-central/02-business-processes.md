# Business Central: Business Processes and Data

Common BC business areas include finance, purchasing, sales, inventory, projects, and service. For an engineering team, identifying **master data**, **business documents**, and **state changes** is more useful initially than studying pages or table structures directly.

- Master data examples: customers, vendors, and items.
- Business document examples: purchase orders, sales orders, and invoices.
- Relationships to record: who creates a document, who approves or posts it, and when data is synchronized to other systems.

Balco can later choose a real process, map the steps from initiation to completion, and identify which steps belong to BC, SharePoint, or a custom application.

Reference: [Business Central functional documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/dynamics365/business-central/).
